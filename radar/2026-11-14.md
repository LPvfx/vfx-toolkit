# Radar: 14 Nov 2026

A tools week rather than a models week. Unreal 5.9 Preview goes all-in on agents
(MCP client and server, Claude Code as an agent, Unreal Canvas), Blender 5.3 ships
with splats, and splat-from-frames services from World Labs and SpAItial open
up. The most useful new open weights are RelayVSR, an Apache-2.0 video upscaler,
and PVDepth, which does depth for panoramic video. Items the earlier editions
missed are marked **(catch-up)**.

Same conventions as before: **Mac** means the 64GB M5 Pro, and **(secondary)**
means the item was confirmed only through third-party coverage.

## Top picks

1. **Unreal 5.9 Preview (9 Nov).** UE's MCP tooling grew a lot. There's now an MCP *client*, a toolset registry, access levels for agents, and Claude Code as a selectable agent. Unreal Canvas generates images, video, meshes and splats from viewport renders. Experimental splat import is on the roadmap. It's a preview, so keep it off real projects.
2. **RelayVSR in ComfyUI 0.45.** Streaming 4× (and 2×) video super-resolution built on Wan 2.2 5B. Apache-2.0, and native in ComfyUI. It's a second open upscaler to test against SeedVR2.
3. **PVDepth (ICML'26).** Temporally stable depth for equirectangular video, with weights and a synthetic panoramic dataset. Directly useful for 360 capture and dome projection.
4. **Blender 5.3 released (10 Nov).** The splat support from last week's beta has shipped, and it imports glTF `KHR_gaussian_splatting` as well. It still can't export splats.
5. **Splats from CG renders.** A short guest write-up shows the recipe: headless Blender renders about 150 views with alpha, then gsplat trains each asset in about 20 s. This is the CG-to-splat route for realtime and projection without photogrammetry.

## 1. Video, upscaling and control

- **RelayVSR.** Streaming 4× real-world video super-resolution. A Wan2.2-TI2V-5B-derived model generates sparse reference frames, and a lightweight VSR network carries them across the clip. ComfyUI 0.45 adds native support and a "Video Upscale: RelayVSR 2x" template, with int8 x4 and x2 models.
  - **Why:** an open plate upscaler that doesn't run the full diffusion model on every frame. Compare its temporal stability with SeedVR2.
  - **Open:** Apache-2.0 weights; the code is on GitHub.
  - **Mac:** unknown, as no hardware requirements are stated. Plan on a rented GPU.
  - https://huggingface.co/Comfy-Org/RelayVSR · https://huggingface.co/kopper/RelayVSR · https://github.com/kopperx/RelayVSR
- **ComfyUI 0.45.0 / 0.45.1 (10 Nov).**
  - **Local:** RelayVSR, MiniMax-H3-image (**H3 licence, excludes the UK**), UltraTex texturing on FLUX.2 Klein, and a solid mode for *Extract Mesh from Splat*.
  - **Paid partner nodes:** LTX Video Reframe, LTX SDR-to-HDR, AlphaGen mattes, Kling 4.0 Flash, Seedream 5.5 (with up to 99-layer separation) and GPT Image GIF/SVG. These are pay-per-second API nodes, not local models.
  - https://freedom.tech/posts/2026-11-10-comfyui-0-45-0/ · https://www.creativeainews.com/articles/comfyui-v0-45-gif-svg-hdr-reframe-cost-2026/ (secondary)
- **NVIDIA Cosmos 3 Nano (catch-up, 5 Nov).** Four open 16B checkpoints.
  - **Sim-Transfer** is the VFX-relevant one: depth, segmentation, edges or blur plus text become RGB video. The other three are for driving or robotics.
  - **Licence:** OpenMDW-1.1, which allows commercial use.
  - **Mac:** no. It needs Linux, an NVIDIA GPU and Docker, and is heavy (secondary reports put it around 96GB).
  - https://huggingface.co/blog/nvidia/cosmos-interactive-world-simulation

## 2. Depth, 3D reconstruction and splats

- **PVDepth (ICML'26).** Video depth for equirectangular footage. It adapts DepthCrafter with sphere-aware noise and cube-rectified temporal modelling. Ships with PanoCARLA, a synthetic RGB-D panoramic video dataset.
  - **Why:** consistent depth for 360 plates, dome and immersive projection, and for re-projecting environments.
  - **Open:** code, weights and training code. Apache-2.0 files, but the README says it is "primarily for academic research", and the DepthCrafter-derived parts keep DepthCrafter's licence. **Check before using it commercially.**
  - **Mac:** no. A 110-frame 1024×512 clip takes about 31GB of VRAM, or about 10GB (slowly) with sequential offload. A 48GB card is comfortable.
  - https://github.com/ChuanxinSong/PVDepth · https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07873
- **Blender 5.3 (released 10 Nov).** Splats import from PLY, SPZ and glTF into the point-cloud data-block. They're processed in Geometry Nodes and render in Workbench, EEVEE and Cycles. Relighting is rudimentary: emission by default, or swap in a BSDF. **No splat export.**
  - **Mac:** yes. Workbench also gains ray-traced shadows on Metal.
  - https://developer.blender.org/docs/release_notes/5.3/ · https://digitalproduction.com/2026/11/11/blender-5-3-splat-happens-and-everything-else-gets-better/
- **Training splats from renders, not photos (11 Nov).** A game developer's recipe:
  - Headless Blender renders 120–200 views at 1024² with alpha, about 30 s per asset.
  - gsplat (MCMC strategy, direct RGB rather than SH) trains 6k steps at 400² in about 21 s on an RTX 3060.
  - Output goes out as SPZ.
  - **Why:** known cameras mean no COLMAP, so this is a clean way to make splat versions of CG assets.
  - **Mac:** likely, with gsplat-mlx.
  - https://radiancefields.com/training-gaussian-splats-from-renders-not-photos · https://aster-master.com/devlog/gaussian-splats-destruction/ (secondary)
- **Frankensplat (OfflineTools, 13 Nov).** A free in-browser 3DGS trainer on WebGPU. Takes images plus COLMAP sparse, exports PLY and resumes from checkpoints. Data stays local.
  - **Benchmark:** its own figures on an RTX 3070 claim about 42% faster than LichtFeld, at about 0.9 dB lower PSNR.
  - **Licence:** none stated. **Mac:** likely, via WebGPU, but untested.
  - https://offlinetools.io/fsp-product (secondary)
- **Splat-from-frames services (paid, closed):**
  - **World Labs Atlas (13 Nov)** is open to all. Posed images become SPZ splats ($5 for a refined run of up to 512 frames, 15–25 min), with splats-to-mesh and relight tools. https://www.worldlabs.ai/atlas (secondary)
  - **SpAItial Echo-3 API (11 Nov)** is now open to every developer account. ZIPs of up to 5,000 frames become SPZ/SOG/PLY plus a floorplan, for about $19 per 1,000 frames. https://docs.spaitial.ai/api/capture-worlds (secondary)
  - **FreeGaussian (12 Nov)** processes raw DJI Osmo 360 `.OSV` files and uses the IMU for gravity and metric scale. https://www.freegaussian.ai/blog/dji-osmo-360-gaussian-splatting (secondary)
- **Spaghetti Splatting (11 Nov).** Replaces Gaussian points with blurred line-segment chains, so strands come out ready for simulation. It beats Gaussian Haircut on strand accuracy without a hair prior.
  - **Code:** promised for 23 Nov. Watch for it, since this is a route to groom capture.
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12716

## 3. Image-to-3D

- **Hi3D v1.0 (catch-up, 23 Oct).** Takes an image plus a coarse mesh and refines the geometry (Sparc3D-based), up to 1536 res. **Geometry only, no textures or PBR.**
  - **Licence:** the Hi3D Open License. Commercial use is OK, but it needs "Powered by Hi3D" branding in integrations and a separate licence above 200k MAU or $1M a month.
  - **Mac:** no. It needs an NVIDIA Ampere-or-newer GPU and flash-attn on Linux.
  - https://huggingface.co/hi3d-official/Hi3Dv1.0

## 4. Tools and ecosystem

- **Unreal Engine 5.9 Preview (9 Nov).** Epic says it was built mostly by agents in about a week for the AI MegaJam (12–15 Nov).
  - **Agent and MCP features:**
    - MCP server settings (port, origins and protocol version).
    - An **MCP Client toolset** for using external MCP servers from inside UE.
    - A toolset registry and a Knowledge Base.
    - Unreal Assistant access levels: Ask, Read, Edit, Auto and Full.
    - Claude Code and Codex CLI as selectable agents.
  - **Unreal Canvas (experimental):** generates images, video, meshes and splats from prompts, reference media or viewport renders.
    - Cloud generation goes through fal.ai, Scenario, Luma or World Labs on your own keys, billed per run.
    - Local SAM 2.1 and DA3 steps need an NVIDIA card with 8GB or more.
    - Canvas Bridge exposes Movie Render Graph to agents as MCP tools.
  - **Splats:** experimental PLY/SPZ import with Niagara integration is on the roadmap. Community reports say large files can crash it (secondary).
  - **Mac:** the editor yes; Canvas local models no.
  - https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/unreal-engine-5-9-release-notes · https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/getting-started-with-unreal-canvas-in-unreal-engine · https://radiancefields.com/unreal-engine-5-9-preview-adds-experimental-gaussian-splats
- **DCC MCP servers.** llm-dev `mcp/README.md` is updated:
  - The Unreal 5.9 Preview row.
  - SideFX's **Apex Script MCP server** (catch-up). It was previewed at the H22 launch for APEX rigging and is headed to SideFX Labs, but isn't released yet.
  - https://www.jonpeddie.com/news/sidefx-and-nvidia-bring-mcp-powered-ai-agents-to-houdini-22s-rigging-workflow-at-siggraph-2026/

## Mini projects

1. **CG → splat round trip, fully local.**
   - Render a Houdini or Blender asset headlessly from about 150 known cameras with alpha.
   - Train it with gsplat-mlx.
   - Bring it back into Blender 5.3 and compare it with SplatPatch's training-free mesh-to-splat.
   - Tests whether the CG elements you projection-map can live as splats beside captured ones.
2. **360 depth for the dome.**
   - Run PVDepth on an equirectangular plate on a rented 48GB card.
   - Compare it with DA3 run per cube face.
   - Drive displacement and parallax from the depth in TouchDesigner.
   - Tests whether panoramic-native depth gets rid of seam and pole flicker.
3. **Plate upscaler shootout.**
   - Run the same soft plate through RelayVSR 4× and SeedVR2 in ComfyUI 0.45, and write ProRes out via the 0.44 node.
   - Difference consecutive frames to score temporal stability.
   - Check hair and edge detail against the source.

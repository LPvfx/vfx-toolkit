# Radar baseline: 1 Nov 2026

The first full sweep of generative AI, computer vision and ML relevant to VFX/CG,
covering roughly mid-2025 to now. Weekly editions after this one only list what's new.

**How to read it.** Each item says what it is, why it matters for VFX, how open
it is, and whether it runs on the Mac.
- **Mac** means a 64GB M5 Pro. "No" means it needs CUDA, so rent a GPU through comfy-cloud.
- **(secondary)** means the item was confirmed only through third-party coverage.

> **Licences.** The Tencent community licence (Hunyuan3D, HunyuanVideo, HY-*)
> is reported to exclude the EU, UK and South Korea. Many strong research
> models are CC BY-NC. Treat both as personal R&D only, not studio work.

## 1. Video generation and control

- **LTX-2.5 (Lightricks, Aug 2026).** A 22B DiT that generates audio and video together.
  - Does multi-shot scenes with consistent characters and native 4K HDR. A "DFR" fidelity pass is aimed at EXR output.
  - IC-LoRAs give control from depth, pose, edges, motion tracks, inpaint/outpaint and union.
  - ComfyUI supported it on day one. This is the most VFX-minded open video model right now.
  - **Open:** open weights under a community licence (free under $10M ARR).
  - **Mac:** yes, via the MLX port below.
  - https://huggingface.co/Lightricks/LTX-2.5 · https://github.com/Lightricks/LTX-2 · https://github.com/Lightricks/ComfyUI-LTXVideo
- **LTX-2.3 VFX IC-LoRAs.** Clean Plate (mask-free removal with the background rebuilt), SDR-to-HDR, LipDub, Motion Track and camera control. These are the first open "plate tools" from a video foundation model. (secondary)
  - https://huggingface.co/Lightricks/LTX-2.3-22b-IC-LoRA-Clean-Plate · https://invideo.io/blog/ltx-ai-video-generator/
- **ltx-2-mlx.** A pure MLX port of LTX-2.3 and 2.5.
  - Covers text/image/audio-to-video, retake/extend, LoRA training, IC-LoRAs and HDR.
  - Precision options: int4, int8, or bf16 (bf16 2.3 needs 64GB+).
  - Slow: about 10 minutes per short clip on an M1 Max. **This is the local video path for the M5.**
  - https://github.com/dgrauet/ltx-2-mlx
- **Wan 2.2 / Wan-Animate-2 (Alibaba).** Wan 2.2 (Apache 2.0) is still the latest Wan with downloadable weights; 2.5 and later are API-only.
  - Wan-Animate-2 (14B, Apache 2.0) does reference-driven character animation and replacement.
  - **Mac:** 5B is slow; 14B is no.
  - https://huggingface.co/Wan-AI/Wan2.2-Animate-2-14B · https://howaiworks.ai/blog/alibaba-wan-open-weights-stopped-at-2-2
- **HunyuanVideo 1.5.** Strong open video model with longer clips. Tencent licence, so see the UK caveat above.
  - https://huggingface.co/tencent/HunyuanVideo-1.5
- **ReCamMaster (ICCV'25).** Re-renders an existing video along a new camera path: a generative reshoot. Code is out.
  - **Mac:** no.
  - https://jianhongbai.github.io/ReCamMaster/
- **Uni3C.** 3D-aware control of camera and human motion together. Now a native Wan ControlNet in ComfyUI.
  - https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.14899
- **Video relighting:**
  - **Light-A-Video:** training-free. **Mac:** likely. https://github.com/bcmi/Light-A-Video/
  - **NVIDIA DiffusionRenderer:** de-lights video to G-buffers, then relights. **Mac:** no. https://github.com/nv-tlabs/diffusion-renderer
  - Curated list: https://github.com/houyuanchen111/Awesome-Relight

## 2. 3D reconstruction and neural rendering

- **VGGT-Ω (Oxford VGG + Meta, CVPR'26 best-paper finalist).** Feed-forward reconstruction for static and dynamic scenes, without COLMAP.
  - Reports 77% better camera accuracy on Sintel and about 50× the speed of MegaSaM.
  - Training code and a 1B checkpoint are out.
  - **Mac:** likely, for inference.
  - https://vggt-omega.github.io/ · https://github.com/facebookresearch/vggt-omega
- **Depth Anything 3 (ByteDance).** One model for mono/multi-view depth, poses, intrinsics and 3DGS. A streaming variant handles long videos in under 12GB.
  - **Licence:** Small, Base and Mono/Metric-Large are Apache 2.0; Giant and Large are NC.
  - Native in ComfyUI. **Mac:** likely.
  - https://github.com/ByteDance-Seed/depth-anything-3
- **MapAnything / π³.** Universal metric reconstruction; π³ is a permutation-equivariant alternative to VGGT.
  - https://arxiv.org/html/2509.13414v1 · https://github.com/yyfz/pi3
- **Apple SHARP.** One photo becomes a metric 3DGS .ply in under a second, and it runs on MPS.
  - **Mac:** yes.
  - https://github.com/apple/ml-sharp
- **OpenUSD 26.03.** Native Gaussian splat prims in core USD, so splats can be pipeline-safe scene description.
  - https://aousd.org/blog/openusd-v26-03/
- **Houdini 22 (GA July 2026).** Native splats throughout:
  - Training through TOPs, so it can farm out.
  - Copernicus rasterises splats to colour and depth.
  - Splats deform with Bone and Surface Deform.
  - Karma renders can be turned back into 3DGS.
  - A Labs relight prototype exists.
  - No structure-from-motion inside Houdini, which is where VGGT-Ω or DA3 slot in.
  - https://radiancefields.com/houdini-22-ships-making-native-gaussian-splats-generally-available
- **GSOPs 3.0 (CG Nomads).** A Houdini splat toolkit: render, edit, mesh, animate, synthetic data, SHARP import. AGPL-3.0.
  - https://github.com/cgnomads/GSOPs
- **Splats on Apple Silicon.** gsplat-mlx (training, 2DGS, SH), msplat (Metal) and metal-gauss (MPS rasteriser). All young community projects.
  - https://github.com/RobotFlow-Labs/gsplat-mlx · https://github.com/rayanht/msplat · https://github.com/QuintonQu/metal-gauss
- **4D Gaussians.** All CUDA.
  - USplat4D (monocular): https://github.com/TAMU-Visual-AI/usplat4d
  - Mono4DGS-HDR (HDR from alternating-exposure video, relevant to bracketed plates): https://github.com/LiuJF1226/Mono4DGS-HDR
  - 4C4D (four cameras, suits witness cams): https://github.com/yangzf-1023/4C4D

## 3. Image-to-3D and assets

- **TRELLIS.2 (Microsoft).** 4B image-to-3D producing up to 1536³ with PBR channels.
  - **Licence:** MIT code, but the DINOv3 encoder makes outputs commercial-conditional.
  - **Mac:** yes, via a community Metal port.
  - https://github.com/microsoft/TRELLIS.2
- **Pixal3D (Tencent ARC, SIGGRAPH'26).** Pixel-aligned 3D with PBR, built on TRELLIS.2. MIT plus DINOv3 terms.
  - **Mac:** yes, via a Mac port. ComfyUI nodes exist.
  - https://github.com/TencentARC/Pixal3D · https://github.com/pawel-mazurkiewicz/Pixal3D-mac
- **SAM 3D Objects (Meta).** One image becomes posed, textured objects plus layout.
  - https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam-3d-objects
- **AssetFurnace.** One local pipeline over Pixal3D, TRELLIS.2, Hunyuan3D and SF3D, with auto-rigging. Its README has a good per-model licence table.
  - https://github.com/Bingeljell/image-to-3dlab
- **PBR from images.** Ubisoft CHORD (open source) and SuperMat.
  - https://github.com/ubisoft/ubisoft-laforge-chord · https://hyj542682306.github.io/SuperMat/

## 4. Classic VFX tasks

- **SAM 3 / 3.1 (Meta).** Text- or exemplar-prompted segmentation and tracking across video, so you can "segment every car". Gated SAM licence.
  - **Mac:** likely, via the Ultralytics port.
  - https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam3
- **MatAnyone 2 (CVPR'26).** Human video matting that gets proper hair edges from a first-frame mask. Non-commercial.
  - **Mac:** yes, via a Core ML port.
  - https://github.com/pq-yang/MatAnyone2
- **Object and effect removal:**
  - **Netflix VOID:** removes an object plus its shadows, reflections and physics. Apache 2.0, needs 40GB+. https://github.com/netflix/void-model
  - **EffectErase:** CC BY-NC. https://huggingface.co/FudanCVL/EffectErase
  - Also the LTX Clean Plate IC-LoRA (section 1).
- **Marigold V2 (SIGGRAPH Asia'26).** One-step depth (including see-through depth), normals and albedo from a Qwen-Image-Edit LoRA. Apache 2.0.
  - https://github.com/huawei-bayerlab/marigold-v2
- **Video depth.** Video Depth Anything gives temporally consistent depth. There's also a leaderboard repo.
  - https://github.com/AIVFI/Video-Depth-Estimation-Rankings-and-2D-to-3D-Video-Conversion-Rankings
- **V-RGBX (CVPR'26).** Splits video into albedo, normals, material and irradiance, so you can edit keyframes and re-render. NC weights.
  - https://github.com/Aleafy/V-RGBX
- **Tracking.** AllTracker (dense, high-res) and TAPNext++ (DeepMind).
  - https://arxiv.org/html/2506.07310 · https://github.com/google-deepmind/tapnet
- **SeedVR2.** One-step video restoration and upscale, native in ComfyUI.
  - https://docs.comfy.org/tutorials/utility/seedvr2

## 5. Humans and faces

- **SAM 3D Body + MHR (Meta).** Robust single-image body mesh recovery. The MHR rig has a permissive commercial licence. Native in ComfyUI.
  - https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam-3d-body · https://github.com/facebookresearch/MHR
- **HY-Motion 1.0.** Text to 3D human motion. Tencent licence.
  - https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/HY-Motion-1.0
- **Wan-Animate-2 / SCAIL-2.** Reference- or pose-driven character replacement.
  - https://huggingface.co/zai-org/SCAIL-2
- **Pixel3DMM (TUM, ICLR'26).** A robust monocular FLAME tracker, with a ComfyUI node.
  - https://github.com/A043-studios/comfyui-pixel3dmm
- **Gaussian head avatars.** InstantGaussianAvatar (100+ FPS), Avat3r and LAM.
  - https://github.com/RaymondJiangkw/InstantGaussianAvatar · https://tobias-kirschstein.github.io/avat3r/
- **FLAME-Universe.** An index of FLAME code, data and papers.
  - https://github.com/TimoBolkart/FLAME-Universe

## 6. World models

- **HY-World 2.0 / 2.1 (Tencent).** Text, image or video becomes navigable 3DGS and mesh worlds. WorldMirror 2.0 is a 1.2B feed-forward reconstructor. Tencent licence.
  - https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/HY-World-2.0
- **WorldPlay2.** A real-time walkable world from a single image, on Wan2.2. CC BY-NC, and needs 8 GPUs.
  - https://github.com/WorldPlay2/WorldPlay2
- **Matrix-Game 3.0 (MIT)** and **LingBot-World (Apache 2.0).** Interactive streaming world models.
  - https://github.com/SkyworkAI/Matrix-Game · https://github.com/robbyant/lingbot-world
- **Paper lists:**
  - https://github.com/EasonTuT/Awesome-Interactive-World-Model
  - https://github.com/ziqihuangg/Awesome-From-Video-Generation-to-World-Model/

## 7. Tools and ecosystem

- **ComfyUI 2026.** Native support has landed for DA3, SeedVR2, Uni3C, Wan-Animate2, LTX-2.5, Pixal3D, TRELLIS2, SAM 3D Body, Marigold V2 and Qwen-Image 2.1 (with alpha).
  - There's also a CUDA-graph "Comfy Compiler".
  - Apple Silicon is mostly handled by community nodes.
  - https://docs.comfy.org/changelog · https://github.com/luix93/ComfyUI-AppleSilicon-Optimized
- **MCP servers for DCCs.** Kept up to date in the llm-dev repo under `mcp/README.md`.
  - Official: Comfy MCP, Blender Lab MCP, Unreal 5.8 (experimental).
  - Community: Houdini, TouchDesigner, Nuke, Resolve.
- **Splats in TouchDesigner.** GSOPs for TD (beta) and Derivative's gsScene Palette.
  - https://radiancefields.com/gsops-brings-native-gaussian-splatting-to-touchdesigner · https://derivative.ca/UserGuide/Palette:gsScene

## 8. Synthetic data

**Datasets:**
- **Infinigen2 Flying Indoors.** 16k stereo videos with RGB, depth, flow, segmentation, materials and poses. BSD-3, about 3TB. Its normals are poor.
  - https://huggingface.co/datasets/infinigen/infinigen2-flying-indoors
- **SpatialVID.** Real video with cameras, depth and motion. https://github.com/NJU-3DV/SpatialVID
- **DL3DV-10K.** Multi-view scenes. https://github.com/DL3DV-10K/Dataset
- **TexVerse.** Textured 3D objects. https://huggingface.co/papers/2508.10868

**Generators:**
- **Infinigen.** https://infinigen.org/
- **BlenderProc.** https://github.com/DLR-RM/BlenderProc
- **Kubric:** Google's multi-object physics scene generator.
- **Houdini route:** GSOPs synthetic data plus H22 PDG and Karma, the natural choice for a Houdini-native user.

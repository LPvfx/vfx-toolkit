# Radar: 7 Nov 2026

A modest week for new models, and a busy one for splats landing inside DCCs.
Blender 5.3 gets native Gaussian splats, and a cluster of add-ons shipped
alongside it. A few things the 1 Nov baseline missed are logged here as
**(catch-up)**. The most important of these is the MiniMax H3 family, whose
licence excludes the UK.

Same conventions as the baseline: **Mac** means the 64GB M5 Pro, and **(secondary)**
means the item was confirmed only through third-party coverage.

## Top picks

1. **Blender 5.3 native splats.** Splats import from PLY, SPZ or USD as a point-cloud type and render in Cycles, EEVEE and Workbench. This is the first free DCC with first-class splats on the Mac.
2. **Derivative TDMCP (catch-up).** The official TouchDesigner MCP server, from Derivative. It supersedes the community server as the one to try first.
3. **Holo4D.** 4D reconstruction (VGGT-Ω or 4RC) drives Wan video diffusion as geometric control. Effectively a reshoot or plate-extension tool, with code and weights out. Needs a big GPU.
4. **StarLightGS.** An MIT plugin that writes splats into Unreal's GBuffer, so engine lights relight them. Directly useful for UE virtual production.
5. **Licence warning: MiniMax H3 and everything built on it** (Viggle Remake, P-H3-Image). The licence excludes the UK, EU, US and South Korea. See section 1.

## 1. Video generation and control

- **MiniMax H3 ecosystem (catch-up; weights dated 2 Aug 2026).** A 33B joint video and audio DiT with a Qwen3-VL-32B encoder. Open locally it produces 768p, 4–15 s clips.
  - **Licence: MiniMax H3 Community License.** Use is permitted only outside the EU, UK, Republic of Korea and US, and authorisation is required above $20M a year in revenue. **Not usable from the UK** without a separate licence from MiniMax.
  - This week's additions:
    - **Viggle Remake (4 Nov):** a pair of rank-256 "swap" and "edit" LoRAs for video-to-video recasting, object insert/remove, and relight or weather changes. It comes with ComfyUI nodes, GGUF/int8 builds (12–16GB cards) and an MCP server.
    - **P-H3-Image (PrunaAI, 3 Nov):** a 33B text-to-image and editing model at 8 MP. Native in ComfyUI.
  - **Mac:** an MLX port exists (minimax-h3-mlx), but it is hours per clip on an M3 Ultra. In practice, no.
  - https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3 · https://huggingface.co/Viggle/Viggle-Remake · https://huggingface.co/PrunaAI/P-H3-Image · https://github.com/PipeNetwork/minimax-h3-mlx
- **Holo4D (NeurIPS'26).** Holistic 4D reconstruction (4RC, or VGGT-Ω plus CoWTracker) used as dense geometric control for Wan video diffusion. Consistent novel-view and long-video rollouts from real footage.
  - **Open:** Apache-2.0 code. 14B and 5B checkpoints; one 5B variant is labelled "commercial", and the weights keep their own licences.
  - **Mac:** no. CUDA 13 stack; the 14B model peaks around 49GB, and over 80GB with dense conditions.
  - https://github.com/NIRVANALAN/Holo4D · https://nirvanalan.github.io/projects/holo4d/

## 2. 3D reconstruction and splats

- **Blender 5.3: native Gaussian splats (dev blog, 30 Oct).**
  - Imports PLY, SPZ and USD into a new point-cloud type, with SH stored as attributes.
  - Renders in Cycles (ray–splat intersection with stochastic opacity), EEVEE (dithered) and Workbench. Relighting is early.
  - Available in the 5.3 beta now, with release expected around 10 Nov (secondary).
  - **Mac:** yes.
  - https://code.blender.org/2026/10/splatting-the-gaussians-into-blender/
  - Add-ons that shipped the same week, all needing 5.3:
    - **SplatPatch 0.2.0:** converts meshes to splats without training, baking materials in Cycles. GPL-3.0. Useful for putting CG into splat scenes. https://github.com/timfennell/blender-splat-patch/releases/tag/v0.2.0
    - **SuperSplat for Blender 0.1.0 (PlayCanvas):** round-trips scenes to and from SuperSplat. MIT. https://github.com/playcanvas/supersplat-blender-extension/releases/tag/v0.1.0
    - **Iconem blender_ogc3dtiles 0.5.1:** streams OGC 3D Tiles with `KHR_gaussian_splatting`. https://radiancefields.com/iconem-streams-gaussian-splat-3d-tiles-into-blender
- **StarLightGS (6 Nov).** A UE 5.7+ plugin that renders splats into the GBuffer, so Unreal lights relight them. Ray tracing isn't supported.
  - **Licence:** the plugin is an MIT beta. The companion trainer bundles the Inria rasteriser, so it is **NC** and NVIDIA-only.
  - **Mac:** no. It builds with Visual Studio.
  - https://github.com/CoSTAR-National-Lab/starlightgs · https://radiancefields.com/starlightgs-relights-gaussian-splats-inside-unreal-engine
- **Corona 16 (Chaos, 5 Nov).** Splats receive scene lighting in 3ds Max and C4D. Also adds SPZ support, clipping masks and per-splat EV. Commercial; how the relighting works isn't documented.
  - https://documentation.chaos.com/space/CRMAX/1436221661/16.0.0
- **SpAItial Echo-3 (catch-up, 27 Oct).** From Nießner's London startup: a "world model" that turns a short iPhone video into a metric-scale reconstruction, up to multi-floor buildings. Third-party coverage says it outputs splats, meshes and points.
  - **Open:** closed. It's an iPhone app on subscription, with an enterprise API. Worth watching as a capture service rather than a tool.
  - https://spaitial.ai/blog/echo-3-release

## 3. Image-to-3D

- **pixal3d-mlx.** A pure MLX port of Pixal3D (no PyTorch), including MLX ports of MoGe-2 and NAF. Outputs GLB with PBR.
  - **Performance:** 512 res takes about 10 min in 16GB. 1024 res takes about 21 min and needs 64GB, which is right at the limit of the M5 Pro.
  - **Licence:** MIT for the port; the upstream Pixal3D and DINOv3 terms still apply.
  - **Mac:** yes.
  - https://github.com/lyonsno/pixal3d-mlx

## 4. Tools and ecosystem

- **ComfyUI 0.44.0 (4 Nov).** Adds **ProRes encoding with chroma subsampling control** (finally a sensible editorial output), a VAE compute-dtype node, and H3 support (VAE, latent upscaler, context windows).
  - https://github.com/Comfy-Org/ComfyUI/releases/tag/v0.44.0
- **DaVinci Resolve 21.2 (5 Nov).** AI-assistant refinements in Color and Fusion, plus expanded scripting APIs. Studio only.
  - Background, from 21.1 in September (catch-up): Resolve Studio now has a **native MCP server**, and advanced Python scripting moved to Studio only.
  - https://digitalproduction.com/2026/11/05/davinci-resolve-21-2-new-touches-more-fixes-and-one-tired-news-writer/ · https://www.cined.com/davinci-resolve-21-1-released-ai-assistant-integration-via-mcp-individual-hdr-trims-and-python-scripting-moves-to-studio/
- **Unreal 5.9 Preview.** Expected the week of 9 Nov, with "improved AI workflows" (secondary, no detail yet). The MCP plugin is still experimental from 5.8.
  - https://guslok.com/en/Unreal-Engine-5.9-Preview-Web-AI-Support/
- **DCC MCP servers.** llm-dev `mcp/README.md` is updated with:
  - Derivative TDMCP (official).
  - Resolve's native MCP.
  - fxhoudinimcp (MIT, 188 tools, tested on H22, runs on macOS).
  - dcc-mcp-houdini v0.45.0.
